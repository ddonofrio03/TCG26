Great catching up with Ethan Loomis and the SER team at the ACP Siting Conference.

The big takeaway from our conversation: getting energy projects approved means putting economics front and center — not just the theory of economic impact, but the real stories of small businesses transformed by these projects.

When permitting authorities, neighbors, and elected officials can see what a project actually does for the local diner, the local trades, and the local tax base, the conversation changes. It moves from abstract debate to tangible benefit.
