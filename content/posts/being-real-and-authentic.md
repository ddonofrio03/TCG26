Today reminds me why rural America is so special.

Neighbors, farmers, business owners, and local leaders all came together for an open, respectful conversation about our project. People asked thoughtful questions, shared local knowledge, and gave us important feedback.

That's what makes projects like this stronger — when communities shape them from the ground up.

As we prepare for the next step with the Michigan Public Service Commission, we're grateful for the chance to listen and learn alongside the people who call this place home.

Rural communities are the backbone of our energy future, and it's a privilege to work hand in hand with them.
